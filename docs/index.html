HUD, UI, and PDA overhaul

0.5.1-alpha · public alpha for testing · 2026-11-19

Your HUD and interfaces evolve as you upgrade your PDA, giving a fresh experience as you progress. If you want to keep a specific look, you can always go into the settings and customize it exactly the way you want it.

The HUD evolves with your PDA

Gen 1 phosphor, Gen 2 smoked glass, Gen 3 dark glass, Gen 4 clear glass, top to bottom. HUD style is Follow PDA by default; pick one look to keep it.

Reference: features

Inventory, trade, loot

  • Search as the last Sort tab; in stashes one field searches both panes
  • Type filters; Group by type, stashes too (off by default)
  • Move all and Take all also take folded items; total carry weight under every Sort tab
  • Right-click works on every box; refusal hints when a drop does not fit
  • Drop onto equipment: a module on the Promin, a filter or tank on the helmet or Air box, a scope on a scoped weapon (they swap), an item on an empty Belt box
  • Patches as attachments: drop a patch on its torn outfit to sew it on
  • Equipment pane with the WD slots (Forearm, Wrist, Bracer, Patch, Side)
  • One box per equipment kind; with two primaries the knife key twice draws the sidearm
  • Weapon chips: canted sights (3DSS), tactical lights, weapon kits and Mags Reloaded speed loaders
  • Magazines show their rounds and the round on top; the loadout is one block of pockets, counted by size
  • Chest rigs: Plate and pouches as boxes beside the rig; pockets turn square only when they would scroll
  • Battle belt: its own box with its pouch boxes, Belt slots, one Ready supplies row
  • Item details: floating two-column sheet, Attachments, upgrade tree, a rig's pocket layout
  • Preview before you use: hover a consumable or an artefact to see what it would change
  • Panes fill their space; on ultrawide the frame keeps its 16:9 width at the right edge

HUD, PDA and settings

  • A HUD in place of the game's own, on its own switch: no PDA, charge or headgear needed
  • HUD style: Gen 1, Gen 2, Gen 3, Gen 4, or Follow PDA (the default: PDA 1 Gen 1, KilTrak Gen 2, v3.1 Gen 3, Milspec Gen 4)
  • Theme: Your faction (the default), Promin, ROMEO or one of 12 factions
  • Screen: Follow PDA by default; Mode, Phosphor and Night pick paper, ink and the night look
  • Every HUD part its own row; Squads on the map By tier by default
  • Name tag of the person you look at
  • Map: filters, pan, zoom, Centre on player, right-click menu; our own marks, or the game's
  • Map mods draw in the look of your screen; Map style Colour shows them as shipped
  • Start on: Home, the Map or the Last screen
  • Text size: Smaller, Default or Large. Icon style: Pixel or Line
  • Settings pages as cards; every setting in the PDA, MCM and the HUD editor in the same words
  • Presets: Default, PROMIN Hardcore, Unrestricted
  • Quick slots fade 4 s after use, or Always shown
  • Talk window and use prompt in the PROMIN look
  • One crafting grid for every workshop mode; bench upgrade trees with path lines
  • Prepare for uninstall: one press readies a save

Reference: setup

Needs

  • Modded Exes 2026.10.11 or later (the installer's own exes are older and lack the map widget; below it the mod switches itself off).
  • G.A.M.M.A. (installer 920), WD 0.9.2 or later (required), DX10 or DX11.
  • The PBR engine build (maka-pbr-test) runs the mod; clear the shader cache when you switch engines. Modded Exes with shader bus 7: everything runs, the bus 8 effects fall back. PR 805 (bus v8): everything, on DX10/DX11 with MSAA off; the DX11 rows need DX11.
  • Install Z_PDA_Redesign low in MO2, below G.A.M.M.A.'s UI mods. zoneOS was PDA Redesign up to 0.4.0-alpha, and the folder keeps its name.

Coming from PDA Redesign 0.4.0

zoneOS adds no item, so a save from 0.4.0 loaded with zoneOS alone loses what 0.4.0 added: its hardware items, its fitted visor modules and its installed thermal modules. The save still loads.

To keep the items, enable the uninstall stub, Z_PDA_Redesign_Uninstall_Stub, beside zoneOS: it holds each of them as an inert "(mod removed)" item. Never enable it with PDA Redesign 0.4.0. It is not in this package; ask for it in the issues.

Fitted visor modules exist only in the save's record, so to keep them, press Prepare for uninstall in 0.4.0 first.

Every player upgrading from 0.4.0 sees Custom on the Presets page until they pick a preset: 0.4.0 stored "Skip what devices show" (now "Follow Wearable Devices") as on, and zoneOS defaults it off. Your own value is kept.

Reference: known issues

  • Settings after an update. A modpack update reset one player's zoneOS settings to their defaults; the cause is not found. After updating G.A.M.M.A. or your list, check Settings.
  • Weapon details. Item details for a weapon lists fewer attachments than its hover card, shows a stray row under the picture, and its description's last line can overlap the properties.
  • First open after a load. In one play test the first PDA open after loading a save showed the game's own PDA. Our runs have not seen it again; tell us if you do.
  • Performance (budget 0.2 ms): the HUD costs 0.3 to 0.7 ms a frame while it is on; the inventory and the map up to 2.3 ms a frame at 1080p while open. Fixes are in progress.
  • Gen 4 name tag. At Gen 4 the name tag of the person under the crosshair draws fainter than at Gen 2 and Gen 3 and is easy to miss on a dark scene.
  • Notifications switch on the next load; the other elements switch live.
  • Map icons: on wide screens the PDA map hides the game's own icons and draws the same icons at their true place. A mark just past the edge of the map may not show.
  • Inventory drag and drop: a drop on one of an equipment box's small chips is refused unless the item goes in that chip; drop it on the box away from the chip.
  • Inventory notices: the game's own short notices can draw over the inventory's condition rows while it is open.
  • Resolutions: in-game checks ran at 4K and at 1920x1080. 1440p, 4:3, 16:10 and Russian have not been checked in game on this build.
  • Russian: machine-written, unreviewed.
  • Mouse: dragging in the HUD editor and wheel and drag on the Map in the 3D PDA have not been confirmed with a real mouse.
  • Saves and uninstall: Prepare for uninstall first; see the README.
  • Report what you find: github.com/estebanaraya/zoneos/issues. Releases: github.com/estebanaraya/zoneos/releases
